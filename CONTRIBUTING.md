# Contributing to vkmOpenGL

vkmOpenGL is developed inside vkmEngine, which pins it at `modules/vkmOpenGL`, and follows the
engine's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/V-KMilev/vkmEngine/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) until
it has a page of its own: open an issue first for anything bigger than a fix, build and test through
the engine, and keep its render tests' pictures unchanged unless the change is meant to move them.
